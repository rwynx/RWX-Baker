TIPS & TRANSMISSION SHORTCUTS

CHANNELS
Multi-Channel Sessions: Switch between active channels or toggle Direct/Group conversations anytime from the view panel. In Terminal mode, manage channels from the left sidebar with + NEW.
EDIT
Edit Messages & Layers: Click any message bubble directly on the chat preview (or in the Layers list) to change its text, swap the speaker character, or delete it.
REORDER
Drag & Drop Anywhere: Grab any message bubble directly on the conversation preview (or items in the Layers list) and drag across rows to reorder them in real-time.
RESIZE
Resize Image Messages: Drag the corner resize handle on any image message in the conversation preview to freely scale its display size (120px – 480px).
SEND
Quick Send & Input: Press Enter in the message input bar to send outgoing messages instantly. Use the bottom dock to attach stickers and Baker emojis.
GROUP
Squad & Group Chats: Toggle Conversation mode to Group to pick multiple characters, set a custom operation name, and easily switch speakers from the top strip.
BACKUP
Backup & Restore: Click Save JSON (or SAVE in Terminal) to download a full backup of your conversation. Simply drag & drop any .json file anywhere onto the window to restore.
EXPORT
High-Res 2x PNG Export:
• Screen: Saves the current visible conversation viewport.
• Full Chat / Full Terminal: Captures the complete combined canvas at crisp 2x resolution.
• Multi-Page: Splits long dialogues into sequential numbered slides with continuity overlap.
MODES
Switch Views Anytime: Jump between Classic Studio (Phone / Tablet views) and the immersive fullscreen Terminal layout anytime via the top navigation bar.
