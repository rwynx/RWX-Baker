How to Create Custom Arknights: Endfield Baker Chats

What is RWX Baker? RWX Baker is a free, authentic in-browser Arknights: Endfield Message Generator and Custom Baker Maker. It replicates the full in-game Baker interface, including multi-channel layout! Craft custom in-game conversations, squad stories, or memes in 5 simple steps:

  1. 01 Choose Your Operators & Squad:

    Select the Endministrator (Male or Female) or any of the 30+ available Arknights: Endfield operators (Perlica, Chen Qianyu, Wulfgard, Avywenna, Angelina, Xaihi, Yvonne, and more). Switch from Direct line to Squad Group Chat anytime to build multi-operator discussions.

  2. 02 Write Custom Dialogue & Story Choices:

    Type your messages in the compose bar, add interactive dialogue choice pills, and easily swap who is speaking with a single click on active transmitter chips.

  3. 03 Add Baker Emojis, Stickers & Pictures:

    Express characters with authentic game-styled Baker emojis, stickers, and status stamps (like "Typing..." or "Went offline"). Upload custom pictures and drag their corner handle to freely resize them.

  4. 04 Click to Edit & Drag-and-Drop Reorder:

    Click any message bubble directly in the conversation preview to edit text, swap characters, or delete. Grab and drag bubbles across rows to reorder in real-time.

  5. 05 Export High-Resolution 2x PNG Images:

    Select your export mode: Screen (visible viewport), Full Terminal / Full Chat (complete long transmission at crisp 2x resolution), or Multi-Page (sequential numbered slides with continuity overlap). Click Export to save your image instantly!

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is RWX Baker (Arknights Endfield Message Generator & Baker Maker)?

RWX Baker is an authentic, free fan-made Arknights: Endfield message generator and dialogue maker designed after "Baker", the in-game messenger app in Arknights: Endfield. Unlike basic bubble chat tools, it faithfully replicates the full in-game Baker interface—including multi-channel directories, squad frequencies, active operator statuses, and tactile Endfield HUD elements. Originally introduced on Reddit (r/Endfield) where it garnered over 80,000+ views, and widely used across community Discord servers for storytelling and memes, RWX Baker lets players create high-fidelity dialogue mockups directly in their browser without any installation.

Which Arknights: Endfield operators can I use?

All released characters are supported, including Endministrator (M & F), all operators and even NPCs! You can also upload custom avatars and set custom names. All new operators and NPCs will be added as they are released!

Can I create group chats with multiple characters?

Yes! You can toggle any channel to Group mode, assign squad units, set custom operation names, and switch active speakers seamlessly. You can send messages, images, stickers and add reaction emojis to messages too, just like the in-game Baker app!

Is my chat saved and private?

Yes. RWX Baker works 100% locally inside your browser. No messages, images, or chat logs are ever uploaded to a remote server. You can also click SAVE to download a complete JSON backup and restore it anytime by dragging and dropping the file onto the window.

Is it completely free?

Yes! RWX Baker is completely free, non-commercial, and open source with zero advertisements, zero paywalls, and zero tracking cookies. By fans, for fans!

TIPS & TRANSMISSION SHORTCUTS

CHANNELS
Multi-Channel Sessions: Switch between active channels or toggle Direct/Group conversations anytime from the view panel. In Terminal mode, manage channels from the left sidebar with + NEW.
EDIT
Edit Messages & Layers: Click any message bubble directly on the chat preview (or in the Layers list) to change its text, swap the speaker character, or delete it.
REORDER
Drag & Drop Anywhere: Grab any message bubble directly on the conversation preview (or items in the Layers list) and drag across rows to reorder them in real-time.
RESIZE
Resize Image Messages: Drag the corner resize handle on any image message in the conversation preview to freely scale its display size (120px – 480px).
SEND
Quick Send & Input: Press Enter in the message input bar to send outgoing messages instantly. Use the bottom dock to attach stickers and Baker emojis.
GROUP
Squad & Group Chats: Toggle Conversation mode to Group to pick multiple characters, set a custom operation name, and easily switch speakers from the top strip.
BACKUP
Backup & Restore: Click Save JSON (or SAVE in Terminal) to download a full backup of your conversation. Simply drag & drop any .json file anywhere onto the window to restore.
EXPORT
High-Res 2x PNG Export:
• Screen: Saves the current visible conversation viewport.
• Full Chat / Full Terminal: Captures the complete combined canvas at crisp 2x resolution.
• Multi-Page: Splits long dialogues into sequential numbered slides with continuity overlap.
MODES
Switch Views Anytime: Jump between Classic Studio (Phone / Tablet views) and the immersive fullscreen Terminal layout anytime via the top navigation bar.
